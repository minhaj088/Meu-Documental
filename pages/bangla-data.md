      The situation in Uttara turned deadly on 18 July 2024, when the anti-discrimination student movement intensified across Dhaka. Protesters gathered around the BNS Centre, Azampur and the Dhaka–Mymensingh Highway, blocking roads as part of the nationwide shutdown.

Witnesses and video evidence examined by the UN indicated that police and RAB fired lethal ammunition from several locations, including the rooftop of Uttara Police Station and an armoured vehicle.
